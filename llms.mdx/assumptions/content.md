# Open assumptions (/assumptions)



| Question                                         | What we're assuming now                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           | When we close it                                                                                                                                                            |
| ------------------------------------------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Are crying and heart rate linked?                | We treat them as two separate signs of the same stress, not as one causing the other. So S3 does not expect one to directly change the other.                                                                                                                                                                     | At Demo 1, on the digital twin: we look at both signals together and check whether they move together more than the model says. If they do, S3 will weigh them accordingly. |
| How do we drive the motors?                      | S4 sends the two 1 kHz PWM signals (rocking speed and rocking strength) on the motor connections of the board. The cradle setup gives the motors their power.                                                                                                                                                     | First cradle time slot, before Demo 1: we measure the load (must stay above 0.8 A) and the duty (must stay under 90%).                                                      |
| Can the timers keep up?                          | We build on the fixed PYNQ Z2 board: its UART and I2C for the communication, its general-purpose timers for the 1 kHz PWM signals. We assume the timers can keep both PWM signals at exactly 1 kHz with stable duty, even while the communication code runs.                                                      | First bring-up: we check both signals on the oscilloscope, before Demo 1.                                                                                                   |
| Do the two measurements arrive at the same time? | Each sensor board cleans up its own signal first: S1 sends a ready BPM value (with trend) at 2 Hz, S2 sends the filtered cry level at 10 Hz. So S3 combines two finished values, not raw waves. The open point: the two values must arrive close enough together for S3 to combine them at 1 decision per second. | COMM-2 test (Section 2.4): we measure how long each signal takes to arrive. S3 writes a timestamp on both values, so if one is late, it shows up on the LCD.                |
